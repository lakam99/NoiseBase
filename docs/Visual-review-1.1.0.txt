NoiseBase 1.1.0 generated raster artwork review

Independent visual critic inspected actual native Ivory, Graphite and Walnut
interface renders and the loaded/processed recording state.

Verdict: ready, no critical fixes. Labels and meter scales are legible; titles
sit cleanly on the bezel; switches remain distinguishable; no obvious atlas
bleed, sprite cropping or distracting halos; upper-left lighting is consistent.
Loaded state: enabled controls, green lamp, filename and print-complete status
are clear. Moving needles stay within the paper scales.

Optional polish noted: darker knob caps on Ivory/Walnut and a brighter amber
waiting lamp. These were not classified as critical.

The screenshots are direct native NoiseBase component renders. Actual VST3
module discovery, state, editor creation and audio processing were separately
validated in a JUCE host. Studio One and physical devices were not tested.
